# Creating and editing Luisa teaching resources

Use this guide for every existing or new chapter, including skill links to a chapter or a subset of its slides. The editable requirements and their scope live in [the register](register.json). Begin with L01–L11, B01–B07, FR-007, FR-012, FR-014, UI-034, BKT-035, BKT-036-COVER, BKT-036-SOLUTIONS, LIB-050-SPINE, SOURCE-051 and RES-081 through RES-086. Read the entries for the specific book and chapter as well. A release-specific checklist supplements these requirements. When applicable owner requirements conflict, give the owner concrete options, record the decision in the register, and continue independent work.

## Author the lesson

1. Open the source, the current published lesson, Pure Year 2 chapter 10 *Numerical Methods*, and the [approved Pure booklet standard](../handoff/BOOKLET-STANDARD.md). Identify what students know before this slide and what they should be able to explain afterwards.
2. Preserve every source question, datum, diagram, assumption, intermediate calculation, check and supplied alternative. Make the smallest clear correction to an obvious error and record it outside the question. Flag an ambiguous source. Where the question is clear, verify each worked answer against its printed data and assumptions. Correct any mathematical error throughout the classroom and worked booklet, including downstream steps. Keep a private editorial record of the discrepancy and check the corrected answer independently. Teaching prose may clarify the method. Define symbols on first use. Set prose as text and each equation in native LaTeX. Redraw mathematical diagrams in LaTeX, including arrowheads, units, ticks, labels, colours and a key for unfamiliar symbols. Transcribe every image-based question and worked answer, and redraw each mathematical diagram. A crop does not fulfil this requirement. When a source symbol or detail remains ambiguous after checking related material, ask the owner with the exact alternatives. Check that each symbol renders in the classroom font. Use AI-generated photographs for real-world contexts. When students must identify a film or another original source, retain its authentic still. In Numerical Methods, keep the still from *21* and its associated clip. Keep the teaching scenario recognisable, supply meaningful alternative text, and keep essential labels and data in the native lesson content.
3. Use the Numerical Methods lesson font, raised pale-blue controls and tips. Use the white canvas to its edges and the available teaching area. Give long solutions readable lines or columns. Keep fonts proportionate, do not stretch text, and inspect fractions, primes, exponents and subscripts at classroom size. Remove imported leading exclamation badges and decorative symbols. Preserve mathematical factorials.
4. Label generic assignments **Exercises**. A question from a named exercise carries its exercise number and printed page in the banner and its question number on the board. Omit publisher headings, source tags such as “[Textbook]”, and exercise-only divider slides. Include each exam-style question from the referenced exercise in book order with its complete SolutionBank working behind **Solution** on the same slide. For Further Statistics 1, use the original chapter presentation to identify the exercises in scope. Include a Mixed exercise only when the source presentation references it. Put each extension after the last question in its set. Use a red **Extension** heading against the navy banner. For a standalone exam example use the banner **Test your understanding** and an understated **Exam style question** title.
5. Reveal the premise or labelled diagram before the calculation that uses it. Complete each part before starting the next. Use hanging indents and a blank line between lettered parts. Keep each question visible while its answer opens. Cover the entire answer before the first click and after reset. Keep the prompt, tips and unrelated diagrams visible. Each click or Space should advance the explanation. Show mark totals only where every mark is earned explicitly beside a step. Use [1 mark] or [3 marks] as applicable.
6. Match both booklet editions to Pure Maths in panel geometry, chapter and page furniture, typography, sequence and numbering. Apply the book colour to the first cover panel and the matching resources-page spine. Centre a white “Solutions” subtitle beneath the title on Further Statistics worked covers (#DB4040). Student covers use the chapter title alone. Use normal navy teaching headers, white panels and the full printable width. Keep the corresponding workspace and fully worked answer in the same place. Give each subpart enough space for a student to write its worked solution by hand. Check the distance from one part label to the next on the actual A4 page. Share spare space between the parts and align the corresponding worked paragraphs with those labels. Draw the blank axes or table the student needs. Printed answer rectangles have a thin flat border. Omit interactive button captions and all hidden answer ink. Print complete worked answers in red. Print two slide panels on every A4 Resources page in both editions, including exercises. Each question and its entire working fit within one worked panel. Even an exceptionally long STEP proof stays with its full question in one half-page. Reflow its working into legible columns and give the corresponding student panel the full available writing area. An odd final panel may be left blank. The paper-building module follows its own print specification.

## Accept the delivered chapter

For each slide, read the initial view as a student would. Explain what the question or diagram asks, identify what every symbol means, and solve or follow the working. Activate each control and each keyboard reveal; check the resulting explanation in order. Reset, then compare the initial image and the physical positions and meanings of controls. Check classroom and narrow viewports, cross-references, hanging indents, contrast and clipping. File counts, image differences and successful clicks cannot establish this teaching judgement.

For Further Mechanics 1 and Further Statistics 1, serve the checked-out site and run `python scripts/booklets/resources-chapter.py BOOK CHAPTER STAGING_FOLDER --base-url SITE_URL` in the project environment. The exporter writes both editions and their individual panels into the staging folder. Compare the results before installing the chapter PDFs.

For an edit or a change of sequence in any Resources module, use the same command with `--plan PLAN.json --previous-student STUDENT.pdf --previous-worked WORKED.pdf`. Keep both input PDFs from the same revision. In the plan, `mapping` gives the old panel number for every new position. Repeat a number when splitting one question board into several. List every new or edited panel in `changed`. For example, splitting panel 3 of a four-panel chapter uses `{"mapping":[1,2,3,3,4],"changed":[3,4]}`. The exporter retains the approved cover, rebuilds the listed panels from the classroom route and renumbers moved panels in both editions. Use the dedicated activity exporter for custom teaching activities. Inspect the staged PDFs, verify the numbers with `scripts/booklets/check-numbers.py` after installing both editions, and open any links on the edited sheets.

Generate each private LaTeX original with `python scripts/booklets/reproduction.py SOURCE.pdf OUTPUT.tex`. Compile it with pdfLaTeX in its output folder, compare every page with its accepted PDF, and verify the positions and destinations of clickable links.

Read through the last visible sentence and equation on each rendered panel. Searchable PDF text can include clipped material. For tables, check the row and column headings, units and totals against the source, and make sure a student can follow one observation across the table.

Open the corresponding student and worked PDF pages at actual print size. Compare titles, numbers, question wording and all source steps, then try fitting the written working into each student area. Check the approved Pure Maths example alongside them. Render with Apple Quartz and an independent PDF engine. Regenerate all affected booklet editions, editable reproductions and catalogue aliases. Check the public, staff and student routes, and respect each role's textbook permissions.

Record the chapter route, slide ID, source, matching PDF pages, acceptance evidence and unresolved findings. A changed source, renderer, teaching sequence or PDF invalidates the corresponding checks. Keep inventory, automated checks and individually completed teaching reviews as distinct counts. Mark an unresolved slide or print page as pending.
