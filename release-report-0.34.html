Luisa v0.34 report

Luisa has 2,947 teaching slides and 62 student booklets. Statistics Years 1 and 2 account for 463 slides and 234 booklet pages. The legacy Maths question library has 3,423 / 3,423 completed questions (100%) and 132 / 132 completed sections.

Development progress · Version history · Cloudflare library · ADA library

Teaching and print checks

  • Answer buttons now show their question identifiers and mathematical prompts. The teaching viewers support individual reveals, background clicks, keyboard steps and horizontal navigation.
  • Statistics exercises, worked answers and tables have been typeset. Six repeated course maps and the final Probability extension have been removed. Statistics now has 463 teaching slides across ten chapters.
  • The 62 student booklets contain 1,489 A4 pages. Each page has room for two slide panels. Questions and diagrams are paired with flat writing fields sized for the working.
  • Restored 43 Pure Year 1 diagram files used by the teaching library and offline chapter downloads.

The revision was checked against the 94 original slide and booklet references in the development brief. Comparisons used the published source edition, individual and ordered reveals, Reset, desktop and iPad-sized Chromium views, and the two-panel PDFs. Checks also covered the library links and offline inventory. The physical Magic Mouse, Safari and iPad remain hardware follow-ups. Codex task token and billing figures were unavailable.

61 original PowerPoint files matched their recorded checksums. For Pure Year 1 Chapter 8, the comparison used the reviewed 39-slide import because the current original file has a different checksum.

On the sports-club question, the allocations use the 121 cricket, 145 hockey and 104 squash players in the question. The sample of 30 comprises 10, 12 and 8 players respectively. Hypothesis-test examples and critical regions were recalculated using the printed question data. The three-part Normal distribution follow-up is original slide 76. The Jan 2013 Q4(a,c) attribution is retained on slide 86.
