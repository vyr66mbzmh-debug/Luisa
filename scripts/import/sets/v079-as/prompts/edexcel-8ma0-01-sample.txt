# 12
A student was asked to give the exact solution to the equation
$$2^{2x+4} - 9(2^x) = 0$$
The student’s attempt is shown below:
$$2^{2x+4} - 9(2^x) = 0$$
$$2^{2x} + 2^4 - 9(2^x) = 0$$
Let $2^x = y$
$$y^2 - 9y + 8 = 0$$
$$(y - 8)(y - 1) = 0$$
$$y = 8 \text{ or } y = 1$$
So $x = 3$ or $x = 0$
(a) Identify the two errors made by the student.
[2]
(b) Find the exact solution to the equation.
