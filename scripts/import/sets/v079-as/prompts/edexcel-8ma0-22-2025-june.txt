One end of a thin rope is attached to $P$.
The rope passes over a pulley that is fixed at the edge of the table.
The other end of the rope is attached to a package $Q$ of mass 3 kg that hangs freely below the pulley.
Package $P$ is 1.2 m from the pulley.
Package $Q$ is 0.4 m above a horizontal floor and the rope is taut, as shown in Figure 2 on page 7.
Package $P$ is released from rest and $Q$ moves downwards.
In an initial model of the motion of $P$ and $Q$
• the table is modelled as being smooth
• the packages are modelled as particles
• air resistance is modelled as being negligible
• the pulley is modelled as being small and smooth
• the rope is modelled as being light and inextensible

(a) write down an equation of motion for $Q$ as it moves downwards,
[2]

(d) use the model to find the value of $K$.
[2]

This refined model is now used to find the acceleration of $Q$ as it moves downwards.
(e) State, with a reason, whether this new acceleration will be greater than, equal to, or less than, the acceleration found in part (b).
